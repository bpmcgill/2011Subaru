Factory premium-audio amplifier wiring

Harness-side reference for the factory Harman/Kardon amplifier system: speaker outputs, audio input lines, documented supply/ground routes, and the unresolved amplifier-enable point.

Important for amp replacement: Subaru’s premium audio diagram gives the speaker and low-level/audio interconnect wires at the HK amplifier. It does not identify a high-current 12 V battery feed at the amp connector. Do not use a head-unit power wire or speaker lead to power an aftermarket amplifier. Use a separately fused battery cable sized for the new amp. The factory wiring diagram does not label a dedicated “AMP REMOTE/AMP ON” pin; the existing turn-on wire you ran to the trunk is the appropriate candidate, subject to meter verification and compatibility with the new amp’s REM input.
On this page: summary · plug key · speaker outputs · amp inputs · power and ground · replacement notes · sources

At a glance

Factory speaker outputs

R316 (B), 10-way: front and rear door speakers.
R317 (C), 12-way: front tweeter circuits and woofer circuits.

These are amplified outputs. Keep each channel’s +/− pair isolated; do not common speaker negatives.

Factory R386 / power-ground harness

R386 is a 24-way factory audio-amplifier connector. The manual shows R386 A10 Orange, A11 Yellow, A12 Brown routed through a 4-way adapter connector; A11 is not identified by name as amp remote/enable in the FSM.

Separate HK amp ground: black/yellow lead from R383 connector pin 1 to ground point GND-06 (WI-62/NAVI-03).

Not shown by factory amp pinout

No verified factory high-current B+ input at the amp is identified in the pages reviewed. Do not assume an aftermarket amp can use R386’s small-gauge/unknown-function conductors as its power feed.

Which plugs are these? Plain-English plug key

“R316,” “R317,” and “R386” are Subaru factory connector IDs printed in the service manual. They are labels, not consumer plug names and not part numbers. The wiring diagrams identify them as connectors at the factory audio amplifier:

  • R386 (factory connector A, 24 positions): the small multi-pin harness plug into the amp, carrying audio/control/other factory circuits. The factory view names its pins A1–A24.
  • R316 (factory connector B, 10 positions): speaker harness plug; pins are B1–B10. The manual location page calls it 10-pole and connected to the audio amplifier.
  • R317 (factory connector C, 12 positions): second speaker/ground harness plug; pins C1–C12. The manual location page calls it 12-pole and connected to the audio amplifier.
  • R383 and R384: a four-position adapter-connector pair between the rear vehicle harness and amplifier harness; Subaru describes R383 as the adapter cord for the amplifier and R384 as the matching rear-wiring-harness connector.
  • i26: the 20-position connector at the factory audio/navigation head unit; not an amp power plug.
  • D12 and D2: front door speaker connector IDs (LH and RH); i158 and i159: side-tweeter connector IDs (LH and RH); D23 and D29: rear door speaker connectors (LH and RH). At the vehicle, match the connector by location/keying; these labels are not plug shapes.

Practical rule: find the physical plug by tracing its harness back to the removed factory amplifier and compare its number of cavities, keying and latch with the FSM. The manual’s pin drawings are connector views, but confirm which side/view is shown before probing or building a mating plug. Don’t identify a connector from color alone.

How to read a factory label

R316 B4 = connector ID R316, diagram group B, terminal 4. R317 C3 = connector ID R317, diagram group C, terminal 3. R386 A11 = amp connector R386, diagram group A, terminal 11. The letter before the colon in a diagram (A:R386, B:R316, C:R317) is the schematic’s connector-view group, not a separate wire.

Replacement installation notes

  • R316/R317 are factory-amplifier outputs; the factory navigation unit routes audio to the amp. Confirm whether the new unit accepts speaker-level signals or needs a suitable interface/line-output converter.
  • Keep each speaker pair separate. Never tie channel negatives together; many aftermarket amps use bridged/floating outputs.
  • Factory front tweeter branches are distinct from door midrange circuits. Preserve crossover components unless the new design accounts for them.
  • The rear factory woofer has four conductors (R317 C12/C5/C10/C4) at R117. Do not treat it as a normal two-wire subwoofer without matching speaker/amp documentation.
  • Before applying power, identify harness-side connectors A:R386, B:R316, C:R317 by physical connector/keying. The manual pin diagrams are service views; do not assume rear-of-plug viewing orientation.
